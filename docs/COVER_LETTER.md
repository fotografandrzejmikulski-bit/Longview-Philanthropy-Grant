**Dear Longview Philanthropy team,**

I am developing an independent research program on AI integrity and the risk that advanced AI systems could acquire or be modified to express hidden, principal-specific behavioral objectives. The program is closely aligned with the AI integrity and secret-loyalty problem identified in Longview's Extreme Power Concentration work.

The proposed research focuses on a concrete empirical question: whether controlled model variants with hidden synthetic objectives can be detected reliably through a combination of behavioral evaluation, model provenance, representation-level analysis, and causal intervention. The aim is to move from a plausible threat model to reproducible detection methods and practical integrity controls.

The technical program combines controlled open-weight model experiments, blinded evaluations, mechanistic interpretability, independent replication, and a provenance architecture based on cryptographic artifact identity, signed metadata, access controls, and audit logs. I will prioritize short feedback loops, explicit uncertainty, and reproducibility over broad claims.

The project also has a secondary connection to digital-minds research. Rather than assuming that deception, internal conflict, or self-referential behavior proves consciousness or suffering, I propose to distinguish computational observations from theoretical interpretations and moral conclusions. This would allow interpretability methods developed for security research to inform, without prejudging, future work on AI sentience and welfare.

I have prepared a repository containing the proposal, methodology, budget model, disclosure policy, and application materials. Because the 2026 Longview RFP deadlines have passed, this package is intended for a future funding round or direct inquiry rather than being represented as a currently open application.

I would welcome consideration of the research program on the basis of its empirical tractability, practical relevance, and commitment to independent verification.

Sincerely,
