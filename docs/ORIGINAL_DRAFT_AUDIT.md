# Audit of the Original Draft

The original attached draft is preserved conceptually, but several claims were too strong or misaligned with Longview's published 2026 structures. This file records the corrections so future revisions do not reintroduce them.

## High-priority issues

### 1. Program mismatch
The original draft presents one two-year “Research Fellowship” integrating Extreme Power Fund and Digital Minds Fund. Longview published these as distinct funding opportunities with different tracks and application requirements.

**Correction:** separate the project-grant and fellowship versions.

### 2. Application status
The original material reads as immediately submit-ready. As of September 5, 2026, the cited 2026 RFPs are closed.

**Correction:** treat the repository as a future-round / direct-contact package.

### 3. Eligibility risk
The original draft states a technical track record and expertise in red teaming, SAE implementation, and related work without attached evidence.

**Correction:** no unsupported credentials are asserted. Evidence must be added before submission.

### 4. Overclaiming deception evidence
The original wording treats “Sleeper Agents” as support for a broad claim about secret political or corporate loyalties.

**Correction:** the cited work supports the narrower claim that proof-of-concept deceptive backdoor behavior can persist through certain safety-training methods. The project proposes extending the threat model.

### 5. Overclaiming black-box failure
The original draft says traditional black-box audits are “helpless” / useless.

**Correction:** layered evaluation is proposed because behavioral evaluation alone may be insufficient for some deceptive behaviors.

### 6. Digital consciousness claims
The original draft implies that forcing models to violate “representations of truth” could constitute algorithmic enslavement or suffering.

**Correction:** the repository explicitly treats consciousness, sentience, welfare, and moral status as open questions and refuses to infer subjective experience from computational conflict alone.

### 7. Cryptographic hashing
The original draft implies that weight hashing would secure governments against digital expropriation.

**Correction:** hashing is described as an integrity and artifact-identity control within a broader provenance and governance system.

### 8. Budget framing
The original draft presents a $470,000 / 24-month total as though it were the direct-funding fellowship specification.

**Correction:** project-grant and fellowship budgets are separated, with the Longview-published fellowship amounts quoted only in the relevant fellowship context.

## Overall assessment

The original concept contains a potentially strong intersection between AI integrity and extreme power concentration, particularly because Longview's 2026 Power Concentration RFP explicitly listed AI integrity and secret loyalties as a priority area. The strongest version is narrower, more empirically falsifiable, more conservative about claims regarding digital minds, and more explicit about independent verification and dual-use controls.
