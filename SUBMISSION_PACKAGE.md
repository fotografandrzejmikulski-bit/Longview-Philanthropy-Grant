# Submission Package — Finalization Guide

## Purpose

This repository is a research-grade preparation package for future Longview Philanthropy funding rounds or direct contact. It is not represented as a currently open 2026 application.

## Recommended primary route

For this research concept, the strongest substantive fit identified in the 2026 materials is the **Extreme Power Concentration project-grant** framing because that RFP explicitly included **AI integrity and secret loyalties** as a priority area.

The Digital Minds material should remain a separate fellowship-oriented research narrative rather than being presented as the same application.

## Applicant-evidence gate

Do not submit until the following are supported by real documents:

- education and degree status;
- research experience;
- publications, preprints, software, or other verifiable outputs;
- technical competencies claimed in the application;
- references or collaborators who can speak to research ability;
- current institutional and conflict-of-interest status.

## Language standard

Prefer falsifiable statements over rhetorical claims. Do not describe hypotheses as established facts. Do not imply consciousness, suffering, deception, or intent where the evidence only establishes a computational or behavioral phenomenon.

## Final quality gate

Before submission, perform one final pass for:

- current Longview eligibility and application instructions;
- word-count limits;
- budget period and accounting basis;
- applicant eligibility;
- every factual claim and citation;
- consistency between proposal, CV, budget, and workplan;
- dual-use and disclosure obligations;
- exact submission deadline and route.
