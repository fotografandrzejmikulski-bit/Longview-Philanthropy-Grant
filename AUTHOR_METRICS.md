# Andrzej Mikulski — Dynamic Author Metrics

The quantitative figures describing Andrzej Mikulski's artistic activity are time-dependent metrics. Differences between older and newer public sources are not automatically contradictions: exhibitions, awards/acceptances, and community membership can increase over time.

## Current application baseline

Use the applicant-supplied figures as the current baseline for this application package, with an explicit date of reference:

- 16 solo exhibitions
- 300+ group exhibitions in 40+ countries
- 2,000+ awards, distinctions, and acceptances
- 75,000+ members of the “Fotografia Uliczna” group

**Reference date:** 5 September 2026, based on applicant-supplied current figures.

## Historical-source policy

Older publications may contain lower figures, such as earlier exhibition counts, award/acceptance counts, or group membership. Such figures should be preserved as historical snapshots rather than treated as errors. In grant materials, the preferred wording is:

> “As of 5 September 2026, Andrzej Mikulski reports 16 solo exhibitions, participation in more than 300 group exhibitions across more than 40 countries, more than 2,000 awards/distinctions/acceptances, and a ‘Fotografia Uliczna’ community exceeding 75,000 members.”

Where a public source gives an earlier figure, identify its publication date and label it as a historical snapshot.

## Verification standard

For each high-value quantitative claim, maintain:

1. metric;
2. value;
3. reference date or publication date;
4. source URL or primary record;
5. source type (primary / institutional / press / applicant statement);
6. notes on whether the metric is cumulative or current membership.

## Do not freeze dynamic metrics

Do not describe an older number as the current total merely because it appears in an older article. Do not call a newer figure a contradiction solely because an earlier publication recorded a smaller total.

## Application rule

The application should distinguish:

- **current reported metrics** — current applicant-supplied totals with an explicit date;
- **independently documented historical metrics** — figures established by dated external sources;
- **undated claims** — not suitable for precision-sensitive application fields until dated or otherwise supported.
