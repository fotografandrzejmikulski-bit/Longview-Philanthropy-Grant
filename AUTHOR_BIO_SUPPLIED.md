# Author Biography — Supplied by Applicant

## About the Author

Andrzej Mikulski is a photographer and photojournalist born in Warsaw who, since 2023, has lived and worked in the Cieszyn Silesia region. His professional life combines work as a miner with photojournalism for the REPORTER Photography Agency. His interest in visual culture began in childhood and developed into mature artistic practice, expanded by interests in film and computer science.

His main artistic focus is documentary photography of everyday life and portraiture, while remaining open to diverse subjects and forms of expression. According to the supplied biography, his photographs are published in daily newspapers, weeklies, and monthlies worldwide. He founded and administers the “Fotografia Uliczna” group, which currently brings together more than 75,000 photography enthusiasts globally, and he serves as a juror on Flog.pl.

His artistic record includes 16 solo exhibitions and participation in more than 300 group exhibitions presented in more than 40 countries. The supplied biography states that he has received more than 2,000 awards, distinctions, and acceptances in national and international competitions. Selected works are held in the Artistic Heritage collection of the International Federation of Photographic Art (FIAP).

He received the title Artysta Fotograf RP (AFRP) in 2017, followed by Artiste FIAP (AFIAP) in 2019 and Excellence FIAP (EFIAP) in 2020. The supplied biography further states that he was the first creator in the history of the Łęczyńsko-Włodawskie Lake District to receive AFIAP and EFIAP. He has also received the titles Człowiek Roku Powiatu Łęczyńskiego and Człowiek Roku Powiatu Cieszyńskiego.

He is the author of the books *Człowiek Roku*, *Tetralogia CCR*, *Światło które zostało*, *Baśń o Pornlandi*, *Cieszyn Noir*, *Ontologia Liczby i Geometrii*, and the comic *Druga połowa*.
