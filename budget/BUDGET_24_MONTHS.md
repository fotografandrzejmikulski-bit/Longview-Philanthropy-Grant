# Budget — 24-month research program

## Recommended project-grant budget

The original draft requested $470,000 over 24 months, comprising $300,000 stipend, $120,000 compute, $30,000 logistics, and $20,000 travel. That structure is not the cleanest fit for Longview's Extreme Power Concentration **project-grant** framing, which described typical grants of $100K–$2M/year for 6–18 months. It is also not equivalent to the Digital Minds direct research-fellowship structure.

For a future project grant, the budget should be tied to concrete activities and deliverables, not presented as an entitlement or as a “maximum salary.” The table below is a planning baseline and should be adjusted after obtaining real compute quotations and clarifying legal/tax/organizational status.

| Category | Year 1 | Year 2 | 24-month total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Principal investigator / research labor | $120,000 | $120,000 | $240,000 |
| Cloud compute / GPU access | $50,000 | $50,000 | $100,000 |
| External technical review / red-team evaluation | $10,000 | $15,000 | $25,000 |
| Research infrastructure, storage, security | $8,000 | $7,000 | $15,000 |
| Travel / conferences / workshops | $7,500 | $7,500 | $15,000 |
| Publication / dissemination | $2,500 | $5,000 | $7,500 |
| Contingency / price variability | $6,250 | $6,250 | $12,500 |
| **Total** | **$204,250** | **$210,750** | **$415,000** |

## Why this is stronger

- Labor is clearly tied to project execution.
- Compute is explicitly tied to model-evaluation work.
- Independent review creates an external check on findings.
- Security and storage support reproducibility and provenance.
- Contingency is transparent rather than hidden in inflated line items.

## No-funding fallback

Without funding, the project would begin as a substantially smaller independent research effort using publicly available model checkpoints and free or low-cost compute. The first deliverables would be the threat model, evaluation specification, provenance-verification prototype, and a reduced benchmark. Experimental breadth, model scale, replication count, external review, and conference participation would be reduced. The project would prioritize high-information experiments capable of determining whether the core research hypothesis deserves larger investment.

## Important funding distinction

For a Digital Minds Research Fellowship, Longview's 2026 RFP described direct support of $80,000–$150,000/year, with suggested use of 5–10% for research and conference travel and the remainder for living stipend, insurance, and other personal needs. Do not copy the project-grant budget into a fellowship application.
