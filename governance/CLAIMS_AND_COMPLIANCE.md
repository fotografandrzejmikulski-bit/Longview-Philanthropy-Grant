# Claims, Evidence, and Compliance Checklist

## Non-negotiable claim discipline

Before submitting any application, replace or substantiate every statement about:

- degree status;
- employment or institutional affiliation;
- publications;
- conference participation;
- prior red-teaming or audit work;
- technical implementation experience;
- references;
- legal/charitable status;
- access to compute or laboratory infrastructure.

A grant application should describe documented achievements, not aspirational capabilities as completed credentials.

## Corrections applied to the original draft

1. **Longview program distinction:** the original document combined Extreme Power Concentration and Digital Minds in a single fellowship-like structure. The repository now distinguishes the project-grant and research-fellowship tracks.
2. **Closing dates:** the 2026 Extreme Power Concentration RFP closed July 2, 2026; the Digital Minds RFP closed July 24, 2026.
3. **Budget framing:** “$150,000 maximum stipend + $60,000 compute + ...” is not presented as the default fellowship budget anymore. Direct Digital Minds fellowships had a distinct structure, while project grants used a different framing.
4. **Sleeper Agents:** cited as evidence for persistent proof-of-concept deceptive backdoors, not as evidence of political/corporate loyalty.
5. **Black-box evaluation:** no longer described as categorically useless; it is treated as one layer that can be insufficient against strategically deceptive behavior.
6. **AI suffering:** no claim is made that internal goal conflict proves suffering, sentience, or moral status.
7. **Cryptographic hashing:** correctly scoped as integrity/provenance verification, not as a complete guarantee of model safety or alignment.
8. **Research status:** the package distinguishes proposed future experiments from completed empirical findings.

## Submission gate

Do not submit until all applicant-specific claims have supporting evidence and every placeholder is resolved. The repository is intentionally conservative where the available evidence is incomplete.
