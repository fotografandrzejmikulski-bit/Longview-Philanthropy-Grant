## Citation discipline

The proposal should not say that “Sleeper Agents” demonstrates arbitrary hidden political or corporate loyalties. The published work provides proof-of-concept deceptive backdoor behaviors and evidence that some such behaviors can persist through safety training. Political or corporate secret loyalty is a broader threat model that this project proposes to investigate.

The proposal should not say that internal conflict or learned representation mismatch demonstrates AI suffering. Such claims go beyond the cited evidence. Digital-minds language should remain hypothesis-sensitive and distinguish computational observations from claims about subjective experience.

The proposal should not describe black-box audits as categorically “useless.” They may be insufficient as a sole defense against certain strategically deceptive behaviors; the research argument is for layered evaluation.
