## Evidence discipline

The original draft contained additional citations and claims about AI welfare, consciousness, “algorithmic suffering,” and prior technical expertise. Those claims should not be treated as established evidence without checking the original sources and documenting the applicant's own credentials. The revised proposal uses more conservative wording where the evidence is uncertain and separates empirical claims from philosophical and normative claims.
