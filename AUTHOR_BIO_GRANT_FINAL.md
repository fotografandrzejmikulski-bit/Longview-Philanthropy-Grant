# Andrzej Mikulski — Grant-Ready Author Biography

## Professional profile

Andrzej Mikulski is a photographer and photojournalist born in Warsaw who has lived and worked in Cieszyn Silesia since 2023. His professional life combines work as a miner with photojournalism for the REPORTER Photography Agency. His long-standing engagement with visual culture also extends to film and computer science.

His principal artistic interests are documentary photography of everyday life and portraiture, alongside an openness to diverse subjects and forms of visual expression. His work has appeared in daily newspapers, weeklies, and monthlies internationally. He is also active in building photographic communities: he founded and administers the global “Fotografia Uliczna” group and serves as a juror on Flog.pl.

His practice has been presented through solo and group exhibitions internationally. As of **5 September 2026**, the applicant reports **16 solo exhibitions**, participation in **more than 300 group exhibitions in more than 40 countries**, and **more than 2,000 awards, distinctions, and acceptances**. The “Fotografia Uliczna” community exceeds **75,000 members** as of the same reference date. These figures are treated as dynamic cumulative/current metrics and should be dated whenever used in formal applications.

Selected works by Mikulski form part of the Artistic Heritage holdings of the International Federation of Photographic Art (FIAP). He holds the distinctions **Artysta Fotograf RP (AFRP, 2017)**, **Artiste FIAP (AFIAP, 2019)**, and **Excellence FIAP (EFIAP, 2020)**, as documented in the supplied biography and relevant public records.

He has also received medals and distinctions including the **Złoty Medal „Za Fotograficzną Twórczość” (2023)**, **Srebrny Medal „Za Zasługi dla Polskiej Fotografii” (2019)**, and **Brązowy Medal „Za Fotograficzną Twórczość” (2018)**. He has been named **Człowiek Roku Powiatu Cieszyńskiego (2023, 2024)** and **Człowiek Roku Powiatu Łęczyńskiego (2017, 2018)**.

His organizational affiliations include membership in **Fotoklub RP**, service on the board of the **Cieszyńskie Towarzystwo Fotograficzne**, membership in **Stowarzyszenie Twórców Kultury i Sztuki PLAMA (Łęczna)**, and membership in **Fotoferia Club**.

He is the author of the books *Człowiek Roku*, *Tetralogia CCR*, *Światło które zostało*, *Baśń o Pornlandi*, *Cieszyn Noir*, and *Ontologia Liczby i Geometrii*, as well as the comic *Druga połowa*.
