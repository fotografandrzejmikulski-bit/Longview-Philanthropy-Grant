## Author evidence integrated

The repository integrates the applicant-supplied biography and current contact details together with publicly discoverable evidence for professional history, international photographic work, organizational involvement, FIAP-related distinctions, public recognition, and historical achievement records. ## Scientific positioning

The proposal is deliberately conservative about what can be inferred from model internals. Behavioral evidence, mechanistic evidence, causal evidence, model provenance, and claims about consciousness or moral status are treated as distinct evidentiary layers.
