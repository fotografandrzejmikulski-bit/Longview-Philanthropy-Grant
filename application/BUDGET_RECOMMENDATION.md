# Budget Recommendation — Future Extreme Power Concentration Project Grant

## Recommended baseline

**Requested duration:** 18 months  
**Planning range:** USD 300,000–450,000 total, subject to the future RFP and demonstrated resource requirements.

## Why 18 months

Longview's 2026 Extreme Power Concentration RFP stated that typical project grants covered 6–18 months and emphasized targeted, rapid work with short feedback loops. The project should therefore lead with an 18-month design rather than automatically requesting 24 months.

## Indicative allocation

| Category | 18-month planning amount | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Principal research / project leadership | $180,000 | Dedicated research time and project execution |
| Compute and infrastructure | $90,000 | Open-weight model experiments, evaluation and interpretability workloads |
| External review / replication | $30,000 | Independent evaluation, red-team review and replication support |
| Travel / workshops / dissemination | $15,000 | Research exchange and presentation |
| Software / secure storage / contingency | $35,000 | Reproducibility infrastructure, secure artifacts and bounded contingency |
| **Total** | **$350,000** | |

## Funding principle

The final amount should be justified from the work packages rather than selected to maximize the award. The application should explicitly state which costs can be reduced or deferred if a smaller award is offered.

## No-funding / reduced-funding fallback

At minimum funding, prioritize threat-model development, benchmark design, controlled experiments, and independent evaluation. Defer large compute runs, extensive travel, and expanded replication until additional funding is secured.

## Important distinction

This project budget is separate from any Digital Minds Research Fellowship compensation structure. The fellowship track is researcher-focused rather than a conventional fixed project budget.
