# Applicant Biography — Grant-Ready Version

## Andrzej Mikulski

Andrzej Mikulski is a Polish photographer and photojournalist whose practice combines documentary photography, portraiture and long-term engagement with everyday social life. Public profiles describe his professional work alongside employment as a miner and photojournalism for the REPORTER Photography Agency. His interest in photography began in childhood and later expanded into interests in film and computer science.

His work has been exhibited in Poland and internationally, including more than 40 countries. Publicly available profiles document more than 300 group exhibitions and a substantial record of individual exhibitions. He is a member of Fotoklub RP and Fotoferia Club, has served on the board of Cieszyńskie Towarzystwo Fotograficzne, and has acted as a juror on Flog.pl. He also founded and administers the international “Fotografia Uliczna” photography community.

Mikulski holds the photographic distinctions Artysta Fotograf RP (AFRP), Artiste FIAP (AFIAP), and Excellence FIAP (EFIAP). FIAP's own public collection record lists his work “Weronika” under the EFIAP designation. He has also received national photographic medals and public recognition including Człowiek Roku Powiatu Łęczyńskiego.

His public record demonstrates sustained independent creative production, international participation, community leadership and long-term engagement with image-making and digital tools. For this grant, these capabilities are relevant primarily as evidence of independent project execution, documentation practice, visual communication and experience operating outside a conventional institutional research environment.
