# Longview Application Map

## A. Extreme Power Concentration — Project Grant

**Best conceptual fit for this project:** AI integrity and secret loyalties.

Longview's 2026 RFP requested a project proposal of approximately 300 words plus a mainline budget amount. The RFP's project-grant track covered projects/programs/new teams/new organizations, with typical grants of $100K–$2M/year for 6–18 months. The RFP explicitly listed “AI integrity and secret loyalties” as priority area 3.

### Fields to prepare

- Project title
- Applicant / organization information
- Key personnel and CV/LinkedIn evidence
- 300-word project proposal
- Mainline budget amount
- Relevant references / links
- Legal and charitable-use information

### Positioning

Lead with the concrete power-concentration threat and practical integrity controls. Digital-minds implications should be secondary and carefully qualified. Do not frame the submission as a consciousness project if applying to the power-concentration track.

## B. Digital Minds — Research Fellowship

**Best conceptual fit if the primary objective is individual research on digital minds.**

Longview's 2026 fellowship RFP described one- to two-year support. Direct funding was typically $80K–$150K/year plus applicable research/travel/compute support. Eligibility required a doctoral degree (or, exceptionally, equivalent research experience) in relevant fields by the stated deadline/start conditions. The application focused on the applicant's research directions, intended outcomes, fallback plans, and career goals rather than a conventional fixed project budget.

### Fields to prepare

- Biography and logistics
- CV with degrees, academic achievements, publications
- Around 1,000-word research directions
- Intended outcomes
- 150–400-word no-funding plan
- Around 300-word fellowship/career goals

### Positioning

Lead with research promise, evidence of engagement with digital-minds questions, and a tractable research agenda. The secret-loyalty work can appear as a technical research direction only where its connection to digital minds is defensible.
