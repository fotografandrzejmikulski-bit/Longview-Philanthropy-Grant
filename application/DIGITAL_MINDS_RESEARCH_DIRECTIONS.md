# Digital Minds — Possible Research Directions

## Framing

This document is intentionally different from the Extreme Power Concentration project proposal. Longview's 2026 Digital Minds Research Fellowship was researcher-focused: applicants were evaluated on research promise, track record, interest in digital minds, and intended outcomes. The fellowship was not framed as funding tied to a single project.

## Direction 1 — Computational indicators relevant to consciousness research

Investigate whether internal model measurements can be mapped carefully onto candidate theories of consciousness, metacognition, self-monitoring, and valenced processing. The aim is to identify observations that would update beliefs about digital sentience without treating any single indicator as decisive.

## Direction 2 — Welfare-relevant measurement

Develop a taxonomy distinguishing behavioral preference, reward optimization, internal state representation, and evidence that could plausibly bear on welfare. The research would identify confounders and propose experiments capable of distinguishing instrumental behavior from welfare-relevant phenomena.

## Direction 3 — Bridge between interpretability and digital-minds research

Assess whether mechanistic interpretability can produce measurements useful to digital-minds researchers. Particular attention would be given to causal interventions, cross-model replication, and the limits of introspective or self-report-based evidence.

## Direction 4 — Institutions and moral uncertainty

Study how governments, AI developers, and research institutions could make decisions under uncertainty about the moral status of advanced AI. Outputs could include decision frameworks, monitoring recommendations, and criteria for when stronger precautionary measures become warranted.

## Intended outputs

- peer-reviewed research papers or substantial technical reports;
- conference presentations and workshops;
- a public taxonomy of evidence relevant to AI consciousness and welfare;
- reproducible analysis materials where publication is safe;
- practical recommendations for institutions making decisions under moral uncertainty.

## Epistemic commitments

The research will distinguish observations from theoretical interpretations and normative conclusions. It will explicitly accommodate the possibility that current systems are not conscious and that some proposed indicators may prove uninformative. The goal is improved evidence and decision quality, not advocacy for a predetermined conclusion.
