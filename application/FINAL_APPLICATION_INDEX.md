# Final Application Index

## Status

This repository is a research-grade preparation package for a future Longview Philanthropy funding round or direct inquiry. The 2026 Extreme Power Concentration RFP closed July 2, 2026; the 2026 Digital Minds RFP closed July 24, 2026.

## Recommended route

Primary route: **Extreme Power Concentration — Project Grant**. The 2026 RFP explicitly named **AI integrity and secret loyalties** as priority area 3.

Secondary route: **Extreme Power Concentration — Career Funding** if the strongest case is an individual transition into this field.

Separate route: **Digital Minds Research Fellowship** only if the application is recast as an individual research trajectory centered on potential AI consciousness, sentience, moral status, or legal position.

## Final submission gate

Before submitting a future call, verify only the following:

1. Current Longview RFP and application form.
2. Applicant CV / LinkedIn and supporting evidence.
3. Eligibility and location requirements.
4. Final project duration and requested amount.
5. Final references and contact details.
6. Legal/fiscal-sponsor information if required.
7. Final PDF/export and consistency review.

No private-life information is required for the research substance of this application. Collect only information explicitly requested by the funder and necessary for due diligence, eligibility, payment, or grant administration.
